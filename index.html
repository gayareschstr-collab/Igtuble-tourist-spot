Discover Igtuble

Barangay Igtuble is a highland community nestled in the municipality of Tubungan, Iloilo. Known for its cool climate and panoramic mountain views, it offers visitors a peaceful escape surrounded by nature and beautiful green landscapes.

The local economy is rooted in agriculture and upland farming, with residents growing a variety of crops. Igtuble's mountain scenery, fresh produce, and rural atmosphere make it an appealing destination for visitors who want to experience the natural beauty and local life of Iloilo's highlands.

What to See & Do

Mt. Tambara

Mt. Tambara is a mountain destination known for its rocky summit and beautiful views of the surrounding highlands. Visitors can enjoy a hike through natural landscapes and take in the panoramic scenery. Sunrise and sunset are wonderful times for landscape photography, weather permitting.

Mt. Tambara — mountain views and highland scenery

Mountain Pass Road

The mountain pass offers a scenic journey through winding roads, rolling hills, and lush highland landscapes. It is a lovely route for travelers who enjoy mountain views, photography, and exploring the countryside between Tubungan and nearby upland areas. Check local road conditions before traveling.

Mountain Pass Road — a journey through the highlands

Local Culture & Produce

Agriculture is an important part of life in the highlands. Local farms grow vegetables and other crops that support the community. Visitors can appreciate the hardworking farmers, fresh produce, and peaceful rural atmosphere while learning about local farming practices and traditions.
